Westermo Network Technologies 2011 - 2022

Software Architect

In my day-to-day work, I had a lead role in the design and implementation of the latest generation of Westermo's network operating system targeting their managed Ethernet switches and routers. The system was built on a fully open foundation using Linux switchdev drivers, and my primary focus was implementing the required features in existing switch drivers, and publishing those changes for inclusion in the mainline kernel; as well as developing a new switchdev driver for a very capable layer 3 switch ASIC, from scratch.

As part of a team of four architects, I acted as a bridge between customers and our engineering teams by breaking down operational requirements on their networks into concrete feature requests for the OS. We were also consulted by product management on questions regarding technical roadmaps, hardware component decisions etc.

Before moving into OS development, I designed and implemented an automated test system for said operating system, together with a colleague. It became the primary indicator of software quality by executing several hundreds of functional tests on multiple hardware platforms every night.
